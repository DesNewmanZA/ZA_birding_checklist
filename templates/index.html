Tickbird

Pentad-based birding checklist

Birding somewhere and wondering what to look out for? Tickbird builds a checklist of the birds recorded around you this year, ranked from most to least commonly seen. Share your location, pick a point on a map, or type in a pentad to get started.

Use it alongside your favourite birding app. Get a feel for what to expect in a new area without having to examine a distribution map, use it to sense-check a sighting, or turn a familiar pentad into a game by seeing how many birds you can tick off!

Your location and checklist progress are stored locally in your browser. Checklist data is cached in your browser for convenience.

About the data

All birding data comes from the Southern African Bird Atlas Project 2 (SABAP2), a citizen science project run from the University of Cape Town and built on the checklists of volunteer atlasers. Tickbird is an independent hobby project made by a birding enthusiast. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by SABAP2. It is free, non-commercial, and keeps its requests to the atlas light by caching results for a day.

If Tickbird adds to your birding enjoyment, the best way to say thanks is to atlas a pentad yourself or support the SABAP2 project.
